# Screenshot Guide for Users

When selling your Telegram group, you need to provide proof that you've transferred ownership to @ethioteletrader.

## What You Need to Show

Your screenshot must clearly display:

1. ✅ Group name at the top
2. ✅ "Administrators" or "Admin List" section
3. ✅ **@ethioteletrader** listed as Administrator or Owner
4. ✅ Your device's UI (Telegram interface)

## How to Take the Screenshot

### On Mobile (Android/iOS)

1. Open your Telegram group
2. Tap the group name at the top
3. Scroll down to "Administrators" section
4. Make sure @ethioteletrader is visible in the admin list
5. Take screenshot:
   - **Android**: Press Power + Volume Down
   - **iOS**: Press Side Button + Volume Up

### On Desktop (Windows/Mac/Linux)

1. Open Telegram Desktop
2. Open your group
3. Click on group name at top
4. Click "Manage Group" or group info
5. Find "Administrators" section
6. Ensure @ethioteletrader is visible
7. Take screenshot:
   - **Windows**: Press Windows Key + Shift + S
   - **Mac**: Press Cmd + Shift + 4
   - **Linux**: Press PrtScn or use Flameshot

## Troubleshooting

### "Screenshot Invalid" Error

**Possible reasons:**
- File too large (>5MB) - reduce quality
- Wrong format - use JPEG or PNG
- Screenshot doesn't show admin list
- @ethioteletrader not visible

**Solution:**
- Retake screenshot ensuring all requirements met
- Check file size and format
- Make sure image is clear

### "I can't find @ethioteletrader in admin list"

**This means:**
- You haven't added them yet
- You added wrong account (check spelling)
- Bot needs to accept invite first

**Solution:**
1. Search exactly: @ethioteletrader
2. Send them admin invite
3. Wait for acceptance
4. Then take screenshot

### "How do I transfer full ownership?"

**Steps:**
1. Open group → Group info
2. Tap "Administrators"
3. Find @ethioteletrader
4. Tap their name → "Transfer Ownership"
5. Confirm with your password/2FA
6. Take screenshot showing them as Owner
